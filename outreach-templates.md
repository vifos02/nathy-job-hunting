# Outreach Message Templates

All messages follow CLAUDE.md voice rules: no em dashes, no rule of three, no generic closings,
no sycophantic openers. Lead with a specific fact. Vary sentence length.

---

## Template 1 — Former Colleague

Use when: you worked directly with this person (agency, company, project).

> Hi [Name], I was going through my network and saw you're now at [Company] — congrats on the move.
> I'm wrapping up a contract at Canva Brazil and starting a focused search for the next step, probably
> a Head of Marketing or senior SEO/content role at a remote-first company. You know my work from
> [shared company/context]. If [Company] has anything relevant opening up, or you're in touch with
> their talent team, I'd genuinely appreciate a heads-up. No pressure either way.

**What to personalize:** shared company name, specific role/project you worked on together if it adds
warmth, target role title.

---

## Template 2 — Peer at a Target Company

Use when: this person is in marketing/content/SEO at a company you want to work for.

> Hi [Name], I came across your profile while researching [Company] — I've been following
> [their product/content/something specific] for a while. I'm in an active search for a
> senior marketing role, focused on remote-first companies in SaaS and tech. Before
> applying through their careers page, I like to understand the team from the inside if possible.
> Would you be open to a 15-minute chat, or could you point me toward whoever handles marketing hires?

**What to personalize:** specific thing about their company you know (a piece of content they published,
a campaign, a product feature). "I've been following [Company]" with no specifics reads as templated.

---

## Template 3 — Recruiter at a Target Company

Use when: this person is in talent acquisition at a company you want to work for.

> Hi [Name], I noticed you're on the talent team at [Company]. I'm wrapping up a contract at Canva
> (Brazil market, SEO and content) and looking for my next role — ideally a Head of Marketing or
> senior growth/SEO position at a remote-first company. My background is 16 years in digital
> marketing across SaaS, fintech, and streaming, with teams in Brazil, Spain, and LATAM. I'm
> bilingual in English and Portuguese. If [Company] has anything in that space, I'd love to know.

**What to personalize:** lead role title based on what you've seen posted. If you've already evaluated
the company and know a specific open role, name it.

## Template 4 — Recruiter at a Staffing/Agency

Use when: this person places candidates (headhunter, staffing agency, talent marketplace like Retalent).

> Hi [Name], I'm starting an active search for my next senior marketing role — Head of Marketing,
> Senior SEO Manager, or Growth Marketing Manager at a remote-first company. My most recent work is
> Canva (SEO content for Brazil), and I have 16 years across agency, SaaS, fintech, and streaming.
> I'm based in Spain until November, then relocating to Brazil — so LATAM and European remote roles
> both work. If you're placing roles in that space, I'd like to be on your radar.

**What to personalize:** if you know what kind of roles they specialize in, name the specific
match ("I noticed you work a lot in SaaS marketing roles").

## Template 5 — Warm but Distant (Industry Acquaintance)

Use when: you've interacted but don't know each other well. Conference, LinkedIn exchange, mutual group.

> Hi [Name], we connected [at event / through X / in the Y community] a while back. I'm in an
> active job search now — 16 years in digital marketing, coming off a contract at Canva, looking
> for a senior role at a remote-first company. I saw you're at [Company] — if you know of anything
> in content, SEO, or growth there or in your network, I'd really appreciate the pointer. Happy to
> return the favor if I can be useful to you at any point.

**What to personalize:** specific connection context (the conference, the community, the mutual
person). Without that specificity this reads as mass outreach.

## Personalization Checklist (Before Sending Any Message)

- [ ] Named the specific connection context (shared company, shared project, mutual person, specific content)
- [ ] Used their correct name (check spelling in LinkedIn profile, not just the CSV)
- [ ] Named [Company] correctly — avoid generic "your company"
- [ ] Lead sentence is specific, not a generic claim about yourself
- [ ] Message fits in ~3–4 short paragraphs — long messages don't get read
- [ ] No em dashes, no "I am passionate about", no "I would love the opportunity to"
- [ ] Logged to outreach-tracker.csv after sending

## Dos and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Reference something real — a product, a piece of content, a campaign, a shared experience
- Ask a specific question or make a specific request ("15-minute chat" or "point me toward the
  talent team")
- Be explicit about what you're looking for (role type, remote, seniority level)
- Mention the Canva context — it's credible and recent

**Don't:**
- Ask for a job directly in the first message to someone you don't know well
- Copy-paste without changing the specifics — templated outreach is easy to detect
- Send to everyone at once — LinkedIn limits and reputation management both argue for batches of 5–10/day
- Follow up more than once if there's no response after 7 days (one follow-up is fine; two is enough)
